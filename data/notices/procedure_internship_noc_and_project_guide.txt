DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for internship letter, NOC and project guide change

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. Internship permission letter or NOC. Ask your department office for the format, get it signed by your Head of Department (CSE: Dr. Kavita Rastogi, room B-301; ECE: Dr. Pankaj Srivastava, room C-202; BCA: Dr. Ritu Agarwal, room C-105), and collect the stamped letter from the department office. It takes 2 to 3 working days, so apply a week before the internship starts. Internship during exams or low attendance is not approved. Internship completion certificate. Submit the company letter to the department office within 15 days after finishing. Project guide or title change. Write an application stating the reason, get the old and new guide to sign it, and submit it to the project coordinator through the department office before the mid-project review. Students with an overdue fee cannot register or change a project until the fee is cleared at the accounts office, Block-D, room D-105. Placement training sessions are held on Thursday at 14:00 in the Seminar Hall; resume review is at the training cell. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday.
