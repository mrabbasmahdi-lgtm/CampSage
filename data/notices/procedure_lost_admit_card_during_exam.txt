DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you lose your admit card or reach the exam hall without it

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. You can reprint your admit card from the examination portal at any time, so first log in and print it again. If the portal shows the admit card as on hold, you cannot print it; go to the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114, and ask why it is held. Common reasons are overdue fee (clear it at the accounts office, Block-D, room D-105), attendance below 75% without approved condonation (see the attendance coordinator of your department), or a pending document. If you reach the exam hall without a printed admit card, do not go home. Ask the invigilator for a temporary entry slip, go to room E-114 with your college ID card, and a duplicate admit card or temporary pass is issued, usually within 30 minutes. Entry closes 30 minutes after the paper starts, so go to the examination office immediately. A duplicate admit card for a regular exam is free; you only need your ID card. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday; during exams the office stays open from 08:30.
