DEMO NOTICE: Where to go for what, quick guide for students

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. Use this guide so you go to the right office on the first visit instead of being sent from one place to another. Offices are open 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday, and closed on Sundays and public holidays. Always carry your ID card and the relevant fee receipt. Fee payment, receipt, pending amount, refund, instalment: accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105. Bonafide certificate, duplicate ID card, name or date of birth correction, transfer certificate: admission office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-101. Exam form, admit card, datesheet, marksheet, backlog form, revaluation, result problem, missed exam: examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114. Scholarship application or adjustment: scholarship cell, Block-D, 1st floor, room D-204. Hostel room, leave, complaint: hostel office, room H-01, behind Block-E. Bus pass and routes: transport office, Block-A, ground floor, room A-012. Attendance error, condonation, timetable, section, project, internship letter: your department office (CSE Block-B room B-201, ECE Block-C room C-201, BCA Block-C room C-101). Portal login problem: student helpdesk, Block-D, ground floor. Library books and no-dues: library, Block-A. Medical room: behind Block-E. Typical order for a no-dues clearance: library, hostel, accounts, department, then admission office. Before you visit, ask CampSage which documents to carry so that you do not need a second trip.
