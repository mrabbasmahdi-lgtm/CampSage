DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for attendance shortage and condonation

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If your attendance is below the required minimum, you can apply for condonation (a waiver of the shortage) with a valid reason such as illness. Collect proof, such as a medical certificate from a doctor, or an official letter for a college event. Write an application to your Head of Department that names the subjects and dates missed. Submit it with your ID card to Mr. Vikas Saxena, attendance coordinator, CSE department, Block-B, 2nd floor, room B-214 (demo contact). He forwards it to the HoD for approval. Approved cases go to the examination office, Block-E, before the exam form is filled. Apply well before the exam form date. Decisions usually take 3 to 5 working days. Condonation is not guaranteed.
