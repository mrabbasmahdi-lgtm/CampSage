DEMO NOTICE: Hostel rules and fees

Hostel fee. The sample hostel fee is Rs 18,000 per semester, including mess charges. A refundable security deposit of Rs 6,000 is paid once at admission.

Timings. Hostel curfew is 22:00 on weekdays and 23:00 on weekends. Late entry needs prior written permission from the warden.

Visitors. Visitors are allowed in the visitor lounge from 16:00 to 18:00 only.

Leave. For a stay outside the hostel overnight, the student must submit the leave form on the portal and inform the warden at least one day earlier.

Room change. Room change requests can be made in the first two weeks of each semester through the warden's office.

Mess. The mess serves breakfast 07:30-09:00, lunch 12:30-14:30 and dinner 19:30-21:30. Mess feedback can be given to the mess committee every Saturday.

Complaints. Maintenance complaints are raised on the hostel desk and resolved within 3 working days. This is sample data for the CampSage prototype.
