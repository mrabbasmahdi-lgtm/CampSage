DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when your attendance on the portal is wrong

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If the portal shows you absent on a day you attended class, report it within 7 days of the Friday update, because older entries need extra approval. Step 1: note the subject, date, period and the teacher. Step 2: talk to the subject teacher and ask them to correct the entry in the attendance register. Step 3: if the teacher agrees, submit a short written application with the details to the attendance coordinator of your department. For CSE this is Mr. Vikas Saxena, Block-B, 2nd floor, room B-214 (demo contact); for ECE and BCA, ask at the department office, Block-C, room C-201 for ECE and room C-101 for BCA. Step 4: the coordinator verifies the register and updates the portal, normally within 3 working days. Step 5: check the portal after the next Friday update. If the problem is not solved in 7 days, take the application to the Head of Department. Do this well before the exam form date, because the examination office decides eligibility using the attendance on the portal. Attendance lost because of a college event is not a mistake; for that, get an official letter from the event organiser and apply for condonation.
