DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you lose your ID card

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you lose your student ID card, first inform your class coordinator. Then go to the admission office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-101, and meet Mr. Harish Pandey, Admission Officer (demo contact). Write a short application saying the card was lost. Carry one passport photo, a photocopy of your fee receipt and a government photo ID. Pay the duplicate ID card fee at the accounts office, Block-D, room D-105, and bring the receipt back. If someone could misuse the card, also tell the college security office at the main gate. A duplicate ID card is usually ready in 3 to 5 working days. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
