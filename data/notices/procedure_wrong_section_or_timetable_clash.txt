DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you are in the wrong section, have a timetable clash or cannot see a subject

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If a class on your timetable is not on the portal, you are placed in the wrong section, or two classes are at the same time, do this in the first two weeks of the semester. Step 1: write the subject code, section and the problem on a sheet. Step 2: go to your department office (CSE room B-201, ECE room C-201, BCA room C-101) and ask for the timetable coordinator. Step 3: submit the sheet with your ID card and the latest fee receipt; section changes are only made for students without dues. Step 4: the coordinator updates the portal in about 3 working days. Meanwhile attend the section where your friends are placed and tell the teacher, so your attendance is marked by hand and can be added later. For an elective that does not appear after registration, show the elective registration confirmation to Dr. Shalini Awasthi, electives coordinator, Block-B, 2nd floor, room B-210 (CSE, demo contact). Do not wait until the exam form; missing classes cannot be recovered after the Friday attendance update. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday.
