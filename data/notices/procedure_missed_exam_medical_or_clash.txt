DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you miss an exam or two exams clash

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. Missed exam because of illness or emergency. Inform the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114, within 3 working days of the missed paper. Carry a medical certificate from a registered doctor or other proof, your admit card, your ID card and a written application to the Controller of Examinations. If the reason is accepted you may be allowed a special exam, which is arranged with the next backlog examination. If you do not report within 3 working days the paper is treated as absent and becomes a backlog. Two exams on the same date and time. Do not choose one yourself. Take a printout of the datesheet showing the clash to the examination office, room E-114, as soon as the datesheet is published and no later than 5 days before the first paper. The office arranges the second paper at a different time on the same day or the next working day, and issues a corrected admit card. Wrong exam date on your admit card. Compare it with the datesheet notice and report the difference at room E-114 before the exam, never after it. Dr. Anil Mathur, Deputy Controller of Examinations, ext. 461 (demo). Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
