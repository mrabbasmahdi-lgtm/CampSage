DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you paid a fee twice or paid the wrong amount

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If money was deducted twice or you paid more than needed, do not pay again to fix it. Step 1: collect both payment receipts or bank messages and note the transaction numbers. Step 2: go to the accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105 (Mr. Rakesh Tiwari, Accounts Officer, ext. 451, demo contact), with your ID card. Step 3: submit a written refund request: "To the Accounts Officer. I paid (amount) twice on (date), receipts (numbers). Please refund the extra amount to my original account. Name, class, student ID." Step 4: the accounts office verifies with the bank and the refund reaches the original payment account in 10 to 15 working days. If an amount was deducted from the bank but no receipt was generated, wait 48 hours and then bring the bank message to the same office. Extra money paid is not adjusted into the next semester unless you ask for it in writing. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
