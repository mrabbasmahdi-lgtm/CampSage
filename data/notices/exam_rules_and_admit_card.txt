DEMO NOTICE: Exam rules and admit card

Admit card. Admit cards are available on the examination portal from 7 days before the first paper. Students must carry a printed admit card and the college ID card to every paper. Students without an admit card are not allowed into the hall.

Admit card on hold. An admit card is held if the student has overdue fees, attendance below 75% without an approved condonation, or pending documents. The student must clear the issue at the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114.

Reporting time. Students must reach the exam hall 30 minutes before the start of the paper. Entry is closed 30 minutes after the paper starts.

Allowed items. Pen, pencil, transparent geometry box and a basic non-programmable calculator where the question paper permits. Mobile phones, smart watches and notes are not allowed and their use is treated as unfair means.

Seating. The seat number appears on the admit card and the seating plan is displayed outside each hall.

Results. Results are declared within 30 days of the last paper on the examination portal. This is sample data for the CampSage prototype.
