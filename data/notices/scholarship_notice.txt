DEMO NOTICE: Scholarship applications

Merit scholarship. Students with a CGPA of 8.0 or above in the previous academic year and no active backlog can apply for the merit scholarship. A CGPA of 9.0 or above receives a 50% tuition waiver. Renewal needs a CGPA of at least 8.5 and 85% attendance.

Need-based scholarship. Students whose family annual income is below Rs 4,00,000 can apply with an income certificate. The scholarship covers up to 30% of the tuition fee.

Deadline. Applications close on 10 November 2026 on the student portal. Upload the mark sheet, income certificate where needed, and a bank passbook copy.

Results. The scholarship list is published on 25 November 2026 and the waiver is adjusted in the next fee instalment.

Students with an active backlog or with attendance below 75% are not eligible for the merit scholarship. This is sample data for the CampSage prototype.
