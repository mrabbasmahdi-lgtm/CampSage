DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when your result shows absent, marks missing or a wrong subject

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If your result shows AB (absent) although you appeared, shows zero, has marks missing, or lists a subject you did not take, do not wait for it to fix itself. Step 1: take a screenshot of the result and collect your admit card and, if you have it, the attendance sheet signature copy. Step 2: tell your class coordinator or department office first and ask them to confirm in writing that you appeared; for CSE this is the department office, Block-B, 2nd floor, room B-201. Step 3: write an application to the Controller of Examinations stating your name, student ID, class, subject, exam date and the problem. Step 4: submit it with the admit card, your ID card and a photocopy of the result at the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114 (Dr. Anil Mathur, Deputy Controller of Examinations, demo contact). Sample application: "To the Controller of Examinations. In the (month, year) examination I appeared in (subject) on (date). My result shows (problem). Please verify my answer sheet and attendance record and correct the result. Name, class, student ID." Step 5: ask for an acknowledgement slip with a reference number. The usual resolution time is 5 to 7 working days. Report the problem as soon as the result is declared because corrections after 30 days need extra approval. Do not apply for revaluation for this problem; revaluation does not correct a missing or absent entry.
