DEMO NOTICE: Helpdesk and grievance redressal

Student helpdesk. The student helpdesk is in Block A, ground floor, open from 10:00 to 16:00 on working days. It handles fee, registration, certificate, ID card and portal login queries.

Examination cell. Admit card, revaluation and result queries go to the examination cell, Block A, room 12.

Grievance. Students can submit grievances about classes, faculty, hostel or facilities on the grievance form on the portal. A reply is given within 7 working days. Complaints can be made anonymously.

Anti-ragging and safety. The anti-ragging helpline is available 24 hours. Incidents are handled by the anti-ragging committee with strict confidentiality.

Counselling. Free confidential counselling is available at the student wellness centre on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 11:00 to 15:00.

Portal login. If the portal password does not work, use the reset option or visit the helpdesk with the college ID card. This is sample data for the CampSage prototype.
