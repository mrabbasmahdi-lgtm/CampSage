DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for hostel room change, leave and complaints

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. The hostel office is at the hostel, room H-01, behind Block-E (Mr. Arif Mirza, hostel warden, ext. 481, demo contact). Room change. Requests are accepted only in the first two weeks of a semester. Submit the request form at room H-01 with your ID card and a reason; the warden replies within 7 working days, and silence after 7 days means the request needs a reminder, so ask for the request number when you submit. Maintenance complaint such as water, power or a broken bed. Raise it at the hostel desk with your room number and it is resolved within 3 working days; if not, take the request number to the warden. Overnight leave. Fill the leave form on the portal and tell the warden one day before. Hostel fee query. The hostel fee is paid at the accounts office, Block-D, room D-105; ask for a receipt that shows the hostel head separately. Security deposit refund. It is returned after the vacating form and a no-dues check from the library and accounts office, usually within 30 days of leaving. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
