DEMO NOTICE: Certificates, ID card and no-dues

Bonafide and other certificates. A bonafide certificate, character certificate or fee certificate can be requested on the student portal. The fee is Rs 100 per certificate and it is ready in 3 working days. Urgent same-day service costs Rs 300.

Mark sheets and transcripts. Duplicate mark sheets cost Rs 500 each; official transcripts cost Rs 1,000 and take 7 working days.

ID card. A lost college ID card can be replaced for Rs 200 after submitting an application with a copy of the FIR or a written declaration. ID cards are required for entering the campus and for every exam.

No-dues certificate. Final-year students need no-dues clearance from the accounts office, library and hostel before the degree certificate. Clearance is issued only after all fees, fines and dues are cleared.

Name or date of birth correction. A correction request needs a copy of the school certificate and takes 15 working days. This is sample data for the CampSage prototype.
