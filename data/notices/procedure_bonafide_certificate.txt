DEMO NOTICE: Procedure to get a bonafide certificate

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. A bonafide certificate proves you are a current student. It is used for bank, scholarship, passport and travel concession forms. Go to the admission office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-101, and meet Mr. Harish Pandey, Admission Officer (demo contact). Carry your ID card and a photocopy of it. Write a short application that says what the certificate is for and who will receive it. Your fee dues should be clear. The certificate is normally issued in 1 to 2 working days, and it needs the signature of the Admission Officer. Ask for extra copies at the same time if you need more than one. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
