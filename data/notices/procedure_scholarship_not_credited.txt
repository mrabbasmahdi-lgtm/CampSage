DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when your scholarship is not shown in your fee or not credited

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you were told you got a scholarship but your fee statement still shows the full amount, the waiver has probably not been adjusted yet. The scholarship list is published on 25 November 2026 and the waiver is adjusted in the next fee instalment, so a fee shown before that date does not include it. If your scholarship is already active and the fee is wrong, step 1: take a screenshot of the fee statement and the scholarship sanction letter. Step 2: go to the scholarship cell, Block-D, 1st floor, room D-204 (Mrs. Neelam Chauhan, Scholarship Coordinator, ext. 471, demo contact) with your ID card and mark sheet. Step 3: she confirms the sanction and sends a written adjustment note. Step 4: take the note to the accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105, so the fee record is corrected. Do not pay the full amount to avoid the late fee; ask the accounts office for an adjustment slip. Renewal needs a CGPA of at least 8.5 and 85% attendance. A merit scholarship is withdrawn if an active backlog appears. Both offices are open 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
