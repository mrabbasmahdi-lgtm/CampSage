DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you paid your fees but the portal still shows a pending amount

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you paid all your fees but the portal still shows a pending amount, follow these steps. First check your payment receipt. The portal can take up to 48 hours to update after a payment, so wait that long. Keep your receipt number safe. If the amount is still pending after 48 hours, go to the Block-D admission and accounts office, ground floor. Meet Mr. Rakesh Tiwari, Accounts Officer, room D-105 (demo contact). Carry the payment receipt, your ID card and one photocopy of the receipt. Submit a short written application. Sample application: "To the Accounts Officer. I paid my semester fee on (date), receipt number (number), amount (amount). The portal still shows a pending amount. Please verify and update my record. Name, class, student ID." The expected resolution time is 2 to 3 working days. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
