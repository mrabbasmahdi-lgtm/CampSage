DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you have a backlog (failed a subject) and want to appear again

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. A backlog means you failed or were marked absent in a subject. You do not lose the semester; you appear again in the backlog examination for that subject. Many students miss it because nobody tells them the form is separate from the regular exam form. Step 1: check your result on the examination portal and note the subject code of every failed subject. Step 2: clear any pending fee at the accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105, because the form is not accepted with dues. Step 3: pay the backlog exam fee of Rs 1,200 per paper on the fee portal and keep the receipt. Step 4: fill the backlog exam form on the examination portal, print it, and submit it with the fee receipt, your ID card and a photocopy of your last marksheet to the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114 (Dr. Anil Mathur, Deputy Controller of Examinations, demo contact). Step 5: collect the new admit card after the datesheet is published. The regular form deadline for the October 2026 cycle was 5 October 2026. A late form is accepted until 20 October 2026 with an extra Rs 500 late fee. After that you must wait for the next backlog examination. Backlog papers in this cycle: Operating Systems on 4 November 2026 and Digital Signal Processing on 5 November 2026. Attendance is not required again for a backlog paper. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday. A backlog also affects placement eligibility and merit scholarship eligibility until it is cleared.
