DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you cannot log in to the student portal

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If the portal says wrong password, use the Forgot password option on the login page and a reset link is sent to the email registered at admission. If the registered email or phone number is old, you cannot reset it yourself. Go to the student helpdesk, Block-D, ground floor, next to the admission and accounts offices, with your ID card and a photocopy of your fee receipt, and ask for a portal reset. A temporary password is issued the same day and must be changed at first login. If the portal shows no fee, no timetable or someone else's data after login, log out, clear the browser cache, and try again after 30 minutes; if the problem continues, report it at the helpdesk with a screenshot. Never share your portal password or the OTP with anyone, including seniors who offer to fill forms. Helpdesk hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
