DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for college bus pass, route and timing problems

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. The transport office is in Block-A, ground floor, room A-012 (Mr. Dinesh Yadav, transport in-charge, ext. 491, demo contact). New bus pass. Pay the transport fee at the accounts office, Block-D, room D-105, then take the receipt, your ID card and one passport photo to room A-012 and choose a route and stop. The pass is ready in 2 working days. Pass expired or lost. Renew it at room A-012 with your ID card and the old pass; a lost pass is replaced for Rs 100 and needs a written declaration. Change of stop or route. Apply at room A-012 before the 10th of the month; changes apply from the next month. Bus late or missed. Tell the transport office, which keeps a complaint register. Routes and timings are printed on the transport notice board outside room A-012. The college bus reaches the main gate at Block-A at about 08:45 on working days. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
