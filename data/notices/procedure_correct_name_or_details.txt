DEMO NOTICE: Procedure to correct your name or personal details

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If your name, date of birth, father's name or other details are wrong in the college record, go to the admission office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-101. Meet Mr. Harish Pandey, Admission Officer (demo contact). Carry your ID card, your class 10 certificate, one government photo ID and photocopies of both. Submit an application that states the wrong detail and the correct detail. Name changes also need an affidavit. If your exam records are affected, the examination office, Block-E, room E-114, must be told too. The correction usually takes 5 to 7 working days. Do it before the exam form date so the marksheet is correct. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
