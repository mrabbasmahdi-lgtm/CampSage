DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for an elective registration problem

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you cannot register for an elective, first check that your semester fee has no pending amount, because unpaid fees can block registration. Then check that the registration window is still open. If the portal still shows an error, meet Dr. Shalini Awasthi, electives coordinator, CSE department, Block-B, 2nd floor, room B-210 (demo contact). Carry your ID card and a screenshot of the error. Write a short application stating your name, class, the elective you want and the error you see. If the window has closed, ask for late registration in the same application and have the HoD sign it. Dr. Awasthi forwards approved requests to the examination office. The expected resolution time is 1 to 2 working days. Offices are open 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday.
