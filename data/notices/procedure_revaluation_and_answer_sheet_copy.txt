DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you are unhappy with your marks or want to see your answer sheet

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you think your marks are too low or a question was not checked, apply within 10 days of the result declaration; after that the window closes and no exception is made. Step 1: apply for a photocopy of the answer sheet on the examination portal, fee Rs 400 per subject, if you first want to see the paper. Step 2: collect the photocopy from the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114, with your ID card and the fee receipt, usually in 3 to 5 working days. Step 3: apply for revaluation on the portal, fee Rs 800 per subject, and submit the printed form with the receipt at the same office. Step 4: wait about 3 weeks; the better of the two marks is kept, so your marks can never go down. Revaluation is done by a different examiner. The result appears on the examination portal; if it does not appear by the expected date, ask at room E-114 with your revaluation receipt number. Revaluation is not allowed for practical or lab marks; speak to the lab in-charge of your department instead. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
