DEMO NOTICE: Revaluation and backlog examination

Revaluation. Students may apply for revaluation within 10 days of the result declaration on the examination portal. The fee is Rs 800 per subject. The revaluation result is declared in about 3 weeks, and the better of the two marks is kept.

Photocopy of answer sheet. Students may request a photocopy of an answer sheet before applying for revaluation. The fee is Rs 400 per subject.

Backlog papers. A student who failed a subject may appear in the backlog examination. The backlog exam fee is Rs 1,200 per paper and must be paid before the form deadline of 5 October 2026. A late backlog form is accepted until 20 October 2026 with an extra late fee of Rs 500. After 20 October the student must wait for the next backlog examination. Backlog papers: Operating Systems on 4 November 2026 and Digital Signal Processing on 5 November 2026.

Backlogs and placement. Most companies allow at most one active backlog. Students are advised to clear backlogs in the next attempt.
