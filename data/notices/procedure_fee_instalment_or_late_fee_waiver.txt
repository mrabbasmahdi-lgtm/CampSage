DEMO NOTICE: Procedure when you cannot pay the full fee on time

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you cannot pay the full semester fee by the deadline, ask for an instalment plan before the due date; the late fee of Rs 100 per day starts the day after the due date and is hard to remove afterwards. Step 1: write an application to the Accounts Officer giving your name, class, student ID, the outstanding amount and the reason. Step 2: submit it at the accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105 (Mr. Rakesh Tiwari, ext. 451, demo contact), with your ID card and the last receipt. Step 3: if approved, you receive a signed instalment slip with the dates; the slip must be shown whenever registration or the admit card is questioned. Step 4: for financial hardship, also meet the scholarship coordinator, Block-D, 1st floor, room D-204 (Mrs. Neelam Chauhan, ext. 471, demo contact), who explains need-based support. Registration and admit cards stay blocked while a fee is overdue, so an instalment slip must be approved before the exam form date. Decisions take 2 to 3 working days. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
