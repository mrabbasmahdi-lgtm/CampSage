DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for transfer certificate, migration certificate, transcript and degree

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. These documents need no-dues clearance first, which is where most students lose days because each department asks them to go somewhere else. Do the steps in this order. Step 1: library no-dues at the library, Block-A, with your library card and ID card. Step 2: hostel no-dues from the hostel warden, room H-01, if you stayed in the hostel. Step 3: accounts no-dues at the accounts office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-105, after all fees and fines are cleared. Step 4: department no-dues from your department office (CSE room B-201, ECE room C-201, BCA room C-101). Step 5: submit the application with all four clearances, your ID card, passport photos and fee receipt to the admission office, Block-D, ground floor, room D-101 (Mr. Harish Pandey, Admission Officer, ext. 441, demo contact). The transfer certificate takes about 7 working days. Official transcripts cost Rs 1,000 and take 7 working days; duplicate mark sheets cost Rs 500 and are issued by the examination office, Block-E, room E-114. A degree certificate is issued only after the final results and all no-dues. Carry photocopies as well as originals; offices keep the photocopy.
