DEMO NOTICE: Procedure for an exam form or admit card problem

This is sample data for the CampSage prototype. If you cannot fill the exam form or download your admit card, check three things first: your fees have no pending amount, your attendance meets the minimum, and your registration is complete. If all three are fine and the portal still blocks you, go to the examination office, Block-E, 1st floor, room E-114. Meet Dr. Anil Mathur, Deputy Controller of Examinations (demo contact). Carry your ID card, the fee receipt and a photocopy of your last marksheet. Submit a short application with your name, class, student ID and the exact problem. A corrected admit card is usually issued in 1 to 2 working days. Do this before the exam date. Office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Saturday.
